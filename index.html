About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computational Applied Mathematics and Operations Research at Rice University, advised by Professor Guodong Pang.

My research focuses on applied probability, stochastic control, and reinforcement learning, with an emphasis on risk-sensitive control and its applications to queueing systems and stochastic networks.

I am on the 2026–2027 academic job market.
